The prototype

Desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Released as v0.2.3 with installers for 5 targets.

Prototype and use of AI

What the AI actually does

Plays from pixels

A vision model reads each frame and chooses keys, mouse look and clicks from the approved controls. When it gets stuck it turns away and tries something else.

Works out the controls

Recognises the game by name or by looking at the screen, and picks a control layout: first-person, platformer, top-down and more.

Judges with evidence

The big AI gets the console errors and a contact sheet of the seconds before each incident, decides if it is a bug, and steers the player.

Plugs into your agent

One click connects Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI through MCP. Your agent directs, our local model plays.
