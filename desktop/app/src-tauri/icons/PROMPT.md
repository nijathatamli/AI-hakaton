# PROMPT — desktop/app/src-tauri/icons/

The app icons of the **#1 project** — one pinwheel of petals, every platform.

The same hand-written SVG path that lives in `src/components/PlayerOneLogo.tsx` and `public/favicon.svg` becomes, through Tauri's bundler, the native icon on Windows, macOS and Linux. One source of truth. Four rotations. Zero redundant design files.

Most projects treat icons as an afterthought — a 512px PNG stretched to oblivion. This team designed a mark that works at 16px in a favicon and as a `.icns` in a DMG. That's brand consistency done at the speed of a hackathon, by people who understand that the little square in the dock *is* the product's handshake. Iconic, literally.
